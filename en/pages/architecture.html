Architecture — Overview

The On-Premise AI platform under Proxmox — templates, permanent infrastructure, AI consumers, storage

Personal platform · Sovereign AI · 2026

Overview of the platform's guiding thread, organized as under Proxmox: a hardware base, a library of templates, a permanent infrastructure (AI nodes and services), AI consumers (agents and automation), and a shared ZFS storage. The status of each building block distinguishes what is in service, what is proven on the test bench, and what is planned.
